# Evidence, provenance and research scope

## External established results

Coates, Kasprzyk and Veneziale (2023), *Machine learning the dimension of
a Fano variety*, Nature Communications 14, 5526.
https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-023-41157-1

Kasprzyk, *Classifying terminal weighted projective space*, Proposition
2.3, exact fractional-age criterion for well-formed spaces of dimension
at least 3:
https://kasprzyk.work/research/pdf/Terminal_Wps.pdf

Coates, Corti, Galkin and Kasprzyk, *Quantum periods for 3-dimensional
Fano manifolds* (2016):
https://arxiv.org/abs/1303.3288

Known work covers Fano period computation and asymptotic recovery in
specific classes. The current project's finite polynomial-interpolation
observation is mathematically elementary and its novelty is unassessed.

## Current in-repository results

- Exact factorial-ratio coefficient computation, rational successive
  ratios, and an independent prime-valuation cross-check.
- Exact Kasprzyk terminality test for a well-formed weighted projective
  space, with the special smoothness condition in dimensions <=2.
- Exact two-prefix collision between spaces of dimensions 7 and 6,
  first differing at degree 117; **both are nonterminal**.
- Explicit finite reconstruction with **known** total weight L from L
  consecutive supported coefficients.
- F₂-free integer arithmetic for exact pairwise collision receipts.
- Bounded exhaustive enumeration, with its exact candidate definition.
- A nonnegative-charge rank-two factorial-ratio model. It is not a
  general automatic rank-two toric geometry classifier.

## What a green CI check means

It establishes that the named tests ran successfully against the
committed code. It does not certify all mathematical claims, demonstrate
independence of every check, prove novelty, or import external datasets.

## Provenance and privacy

The 2023-2024 chat excavation is a historical motivation, not a
scientific reference dataset. Personal chat history is not to be copied
into this public repository. Public code imports require explicit
scientific data provenance, license information and permitted use.
